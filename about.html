Who we are

About Arcade Atlas

Arcade Atlas is a browser-game discovery and play site. It brings a searchable catalog, practical game information, and personal organization tools into one place so visitors can spend less time hunting for a game and more time deciding what to play.

What the site is for

The catalog covers a range of play styles, including arcade, racing, puzzle, strategy, sports, simulation, action, and casual games. Search and category filters help narrow the selection. Game cards include available descriptions and player information so visitors can make a more informed choice before opening a title.

The site is also a small collection of browser tools: favorites, one-to-five-star personal ratings, custom playlists, recently played games, Most Played on This Device, Play More Like This recommendations, Daily Pick, Arcade Quests, and compatible public leaderboards. These features have different data behavior, explained below and in the Privacy Policy.

Original work and third-party games

Arcade Atlas includes original mini-games and site features developed for this project, as well as games created by independent developers and other projects. A game appearing in the catalog does not mean Arcade Atlas created, owns, sponsors, or officially represents that game. Where source and license information is available, the catalog and project notes aim to preserve attribution and identify upstream projects.

Some games are embedded or hosted from third-party projects and may rely on external servers, scripts, or services. Their availability, controls, privacy practices, and online player populations are outside Arcade Atlas's control. A title described as multiplayer is not necessarily a live match with other visitors; some games are local multiplayer or use computer opponents.

How the catalog is maintained

  • Catalog entries are checked against their listed local game path before being retained.
  • Browser launches and basic runtime behavior are tested when a game is added or changed.
  • Third-party source, developer attribution, and license information are reviewed when available; entries with unclear redistribution rights should not be treated as original Arcade Atlas games.
  • Broken, missing, or unsuitable entries can be removed, and compatibility fixes are recorded in the changelog.
  • Descriptions are intended to explain the basic objective and play style, not to promise that every external service will always be available.

These checks are ongoing maintenance, not a guarantee that every title works on every device or browser. If you find a broken link, misleading description, or attribution issue, please report the exact game and what happened.

Local preferences and public submissions

Favorites, ratings, playlists, recent games, launch counts, local recommendations, Daily Pick launch streak tracking, and Arcade Quests progress are stored in the browser on the device where they are used. They are intended for personal organization and may be lost if browser site data is cleared or a different device is used.

Leaderboard entries are different. If you choose to submit a score, playtime, or Daily Pick streak, the nickname and submitted result may be stored by the site's leaderboard service and displayed publicly. Do not use your real name or other personal information as a player nickname. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Report a problem or contact the project

For a technical issue, catalog correction, attribution concern, or feature suggestion, use the Arcade Atlas GitHub issue tracker. Include the game title, browser, device type, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not include passwords, private account information, or other sensitive details in a public report.

You can also visit the Arcade Atlas community Discord for discussion. Public channels may be visible to other members.
