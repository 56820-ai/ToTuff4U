# GN Math game imports

Game launch HTML files are imported from https://github.com/gn-math/html. Game-specific payloads continue to load from the upstream locations referenced by each original launch file; those payloads are not mirrored into this repository.

Game names, developers, descriptions, and cover references are sourced from the repository's gn-math-games.json catalog. Individual games may have separate third-party authors and terms.
