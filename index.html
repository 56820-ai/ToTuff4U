Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arcade Atlas ("we," "our," or "the site") handles information when you visit and use the website. Arcade Atlas is a browser-based game site.

Information you provide

Some games offer leaderboards. If you choose to submit a score or playtime, the player name, game name, score, and/or playtime value you submit may be sent to our leaderboard database and displayed publicly on the site. Do not use your real name or include personal information in a player name.

You can use the site without creating an Arcade Atlas account. The site does not ask you to provide an email address to use its game catalog or submit a leaderboard score.

Information stored in your browser

The site may use browser local storage to remember preferences such as favorite games, your player name, and locally tracked playtime. This information is stored in your browser and may remain there until you clear the site's data or your browser removes it. Clearing it may reset these preferences and locally stored totals.

Leaderboards and database

Leaderboard submissions are processed by Supabase, our database service provider. Submitted leaderboard information is used to operate and display game rankings. Because leaderboard entries are public, information you submit there should be treated as public. Database and service-provider handling is also subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and advertising

Arcade Atlas uses Google technologies, including Google Tag Manager and Google AdSense. These services may collect information such as browser/device details, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited, and interactions. Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure advertisements, subject to your settings, applicable law, and Google's policies.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy. You can review Google's Privacy Policy and ad settings.

Third-party games and links

Games may be hosted by or load content from third parties. External websites, embedded games, advertising providers, and other services may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control those third-party practices, so review their policies when using their services.

How information is used

  • To operate the website, games, favorites, and playtime features.
  • To accept and display leaderboard entries.
  • To understand site usage and measure or display advertisements.
  • To maintain site security and troubleshoot problems.

Data choices

You can clear Arcade Atlas local storage through your browser's site-data settings. You can manage Google's advertising personalization through Google Ad Settings. If you submitted a public leaderboard entry and want to ask about its removal, contact the site maintainer through the Arcade Atlas GitHub repository. Please do not post private information in a public issue.

Children's privacy

Arcade Atlas is a general-audience game website and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal details in leaderboard names or other site inputs. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information can contact the site maintainer through the GitHub repository linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website's features or services change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently updated.
